Waiting for valid intrinsic viscosity

[η] = K × Mα; M = ([η]/K)1/α. K = 2.50486 × 10−4; α = 0.647979 ([η] in dL/g; M in Da). Full-precision parameters are used in calculations. Two original samples (350,000 and 3,020,000 Da) define the calibration span, not a validated range. One independent test sample ([η] = 2.63 dL/g; measured M = 1,600,000 Da) has a prediction error of 0.37%. This is a single-sample result. The calculation route is informed by Kato et al., 1982.

Equations & Conditions
t=result_ms1000 ηr≈tt0 [η]≈2(ηr−1−lnηr)c

Use seconds for t and t₀, g/dL for c, and dL/g for intrinsic viscosity. Measure solvent and sample with the same instrument at comparable temperatures. The time-ratio approximation neglects density differences and flow corrections. The single-point method estimates dilute-solution intrinsic viscosity; suitability for a given sample still requires verification. No automatic temperature compensation is applied.
